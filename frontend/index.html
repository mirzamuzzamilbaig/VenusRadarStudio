CPR Physics

Circular Polarization Ratio (CPR = SCP/OCP) measures surface roughness at the radar wavelength. High CPR (>0.7) indicates rough surfaces from recent volcanic flows or mountain terrain. Maxwell Montes achieves CPR >1.5 via coherent backscatter retroreflection.
